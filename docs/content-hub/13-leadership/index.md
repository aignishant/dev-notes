# Section 13 — Leadership & Behavioral

Lead interviews always include behavioral questions. "Tell me about a time you..." — your answer must be structured, specific, and evidence-backed. This section gives you a **STAR story bank** drawn from the work you've described.

## The STAR Structure

Every behavioral answer uses:

- **Situation** — context (1 sentence)
- **Task** — what you were responsible for (1 sentence)
- **Action** — what YOU specifically did (bulk of the answer — 70%)
- **Result** — quantified outcome (1-2 sentences)

Avoid:
- **"We did X..."** — interviewers want to know what **you** did
- Vague results — "it worked better" is worthless; "reduced cycle time 40%" is gold
- Blaming teammates — even when they deserve it
