# 🔥 3-week sprint plan

> 21 days. ~400 problems. 8–10 hours per day. **Only attempt this if your basics are already in place.**

---

## Who this plan is for

✅ You can already implement: arrays, hash maps, BFS, DFS, recursion, sorting, binary search **without looking anything up**.
✅ You can take 8–10 hours per day for 21 straight days (took leave / between jobs / between semesters).
✅ You have an interview scheduled exactly 3 weeks out.

**If any of these is "no," pick a different plan.** This plan is a polish-and-peak schedule, not a build-from-scratch one.

---

## The bargain this plan makes

| Buy | Cost |
|---|---|
| Top patterns covered | Skip advanced topics (segment trees, suffix arrays, max-flow) |
| 400 high-yield problems | No deep system-design (only 1 mock) |
| 4 mock interviews | No deep behavioral prep (1 day at the end) |
| Peak performance on interview day | Burnout if extended past 3 weeks — do not |

---

## Daily rhythm

```
06:30 — wake, light exercise, breakfast
07:30 — block 1: pattern of the day (read chapter, 1 hr)
08:30 — block 2: 4 medium problems (3 hrs)
11:30 — long break, lunch, walk (1.5 hrs)
13:00 — block 3: 4 more problems (2 hrs)
15:00 — block 4: re-do yesterday's hardest problem from memory (1 hr)
16:00 — break (1 hr)
17:00 — block 5: 1 mock-style timed problem with no peeking (1 hr)
18:00 — wrap, log progress, sleep before midnight
```

You'll hit ~7–9 problems most days. That's 21 days × 8 = **168 minimum**. Most people clear 350–400 because re-do counts and follow-ups count.

## Week 1 — Pattern foundations

**Goal:** lock in 5 of the highest-yield patterns. They alone cover ~50% of all coding interviews.

| Day | Topic | Read | Solve | Self-check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arrays + Two Pointers | [Array basics](../02-data-structures/arrays/01-array-basics.md), Two Pointers pattern | 8 problems | Can I solve a fresh easy in <10 min? |
| 2 | Sliding Window | Sliding Window pattern | 8 problems | Can I derive the window template myself? |
| 3 | Hash Maps | Hash Tables chapter | 8 problems | Counter & defaultdict reflexive? |
| 4 | Strings + Two Pointers | Strings chapter | 8 problems | Palindrome variants memorized? |
| 5 | Binary Search | Binary Search + modified-BS pattern | 8 problems | Lower-bound vs upper-bound clear? |
| 6 | Linked Lists | Linked List chapter | 8 problems | Cycle detection without notes? |
| 7 | **Mock interview #1** + rest | Mock from sec 10 | 1 mock + grade self | If <60%, repeat any weak day |

End of week 1: you should be able to recognize "two pointers / sliding window / hash" within 30 seconds of reading a problem.

End of week 2: you know all 20 patterns by *recognition* even if you can't yet write all of them perfectly.

**Goal:** convert "I know this pattern" into "I solve in 25 min flat under pressure."

| Day | Focus | Solve |

## Falling behind?

If at end of day 7 you've solved <40 problems or scored <50% on Mock #1: **pause and switch to the 1-month crash plan.** The sprint only works if you're already at speed. Not all interviews are flexible — but recruiters appreciate honest reschedule requests far more than no-show or bombed interviews.

If at end of day 14 you've solved 100+ problems and Mock #2 was >70%: **you're on track.** Don't add hours; preserve sleep and confidence for week 3.

---

## Ahead of schedule?

If you're flying through, **don't add more problems.** Use the slack for:

- Re-doing previous problems from memory (gold standard for retention)
- Reading the [Patterns](../04-patterns/index.md) section deeply
- Adding 1–2 system design rounds from [Tier-1 system design projects](../08-system-design/index.md)
- Sleep more

Adding raw problem count this late doesn't help. Compounding what you know does.

---

## Final 24 hours before the interview

- **Re-skim:** [Big-O cheatsheet](../01-foundations/big-o-cheatsheet.md), [How to approach any problem](how-to-approach-any-problem.md), the patterns you scored weakest on.
- **Don't solve new problems.** Confidence > coverage at this stage.
- **Sleep 8 hours.** Non-negotiable.
- **Eat normally.** Not heavy, not skipped.
- **Light walk 30 min before** the interview. Calms nerves.
- **Have water + snack** within reach during the interview.

You've earned it. Go.

---

## After the interview

Whatever the outcome — write down:

- Every question you remember
- Where you got stuck
- What the interviewer hinted

Add those notes to the bible's [Mock Interviews](../10-mock-interviews/index.md) section as a personal post-mortem. They make the next round much easier.
