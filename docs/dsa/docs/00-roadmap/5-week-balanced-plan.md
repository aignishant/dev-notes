# ⚡ 5-week balanced plan

> 35 days. ~800 problems. 5–6 hours per day. The sweet spot for working professionals.

---

## Who this plan is for

✅ You have **some prior knowledge** — solved 50+ LeetCode problems before, recognize basic patterns.
✅ You can sustain **5–6 hours per day** for 5 weeks (working professional with 2–3 weekday hours and 8 weekend hours, or full-time student between commitments).
✅ Your interview is roughly 5 weeks away, **or** you want a rounded prep without the time pressure of a sprint.

This plan trades the sprint's intensity for sustainability. You'll cover more ground, retain it better, and be less burned out on interview day.

---

## The bargain this plan makes

| Buy | Cost |
|---|---|
| All 20 patterns covered | Some advanced topics (segment trees, max-flow) skimmed not mastered |
| 800 problems with reps | Slightly less polish than a full 6-week thorough |
| 6 mock interviews | 1 system-design mock vs 2 in longer plans |
| Sustainable pace | You can't compress this further without quality loss |

---

## Daily rhythm (working professional)

```
07:00 — wake, breakfast
08:00 — block 1 (1 hr): re-do yesterday's hardest problem from memory
09:00–17:00 — work
17:30 — block 2 (1 hr): read the day's topic chapter + 2 problems
18:30 — dinner, walk
20:00 — block 3 (2 hrs): 3 problems
22:00 — log progress, sleep
```

Weekend (Saturday): 8-hour block (2 + 4 + 2). Sunday: rest or 4-hour block + mock interview.

If you're full-time studying, just front-load the weekday 5 hours into one continuous session.

## Self-check tests at week boundaries

End of each week: pick **3 fresh problems** you haven't seen, in the topic of that week. Solve them with no peeking, 30 min each. **If you score 2/3 or better, move on.** If 1/3 or worse, re-do that week's weakest topic over the weekend.

---

## Falling behind?

After week 2:
- If <50 problems solved: drop weeks 4–5 patterns to bare essentials (stick to 1D DP + LCS + knapsack base).
- If <30 problems solved: switch to 6-week thorough; you bit off more than your time allows.

---

## Ahead of schedule?

Use the slack for:
- More mocks (target 8 instead of 6)
- Deeper system design (cover 2 Tier-1 projects instead of 1)
- Behavioral story bank (8 stories prepared, not just templates)
