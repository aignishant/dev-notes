# 🎯 6-week thorough plan

> 42 days. ~1,200 problems. 4–5 hours per day. The "standard" prep window.

---

## Who this plan is for

✅ You're starting **mostly fresh** on most topics — maybe 20–50 LeetCode under your belt.
✅ You can do **4–5 hours per day** for 6 weeks (after work, lunch breaks, weekends).
✅ Your interview is **6 weeks out**, or you have no specific date and want a complete prep.
✅ You want to be *ready*, not just *trained*.

This plan is the most-recommended in this bible. It's long enough to build deep understanding, short enough to stay urgent.

---

## The bargain this plan makes

| Buy | Cost |
|---|---|
| Every core topic covered with depth | 6 weeks of consistent grind |
| 1,200 problems with active recall | Less raw volume than 3-month plan |
| 8 mock interviews (incl. 2 system design) | Limited time on advanced/ultra-advanced |
| Behavioral prep included | You must show up daily |

---

## Daily rhythm

```
07:00 — wake, light exercise
08:00 — block 1 (1 hr): morning re-do (2 problems from memory)
09:00–17:00 — work (or full-time study)
18:00 — block 2 (1.5 hrs): topic of day — read + 3 problems
20:00 — dinner break (1 hr)
21:00 — block 3 (1.5 hrs): 3 more problems + log progress
22:30 — sleep
```

Weekend: 4-hour block + 2-hour mock or system-design block.

## Self-check tests

End of each week: take any 4 problems from this week's topic that you haven't seen. Solve them in 30 min each.

- 4/4 → ahead of schedule, raise difficulty
- 3/4 → on track
- 2/4 → spend Saturday re-doing the week
- ≤1/4 → spend the entire weekend on the week, push schedule by 2 days

---

## Falling behind?

If by end of week 3 you're <130 problems solved: drop weeks 5's optional bits (greedy + intervals can be combined with mock #5 day) and consolidate.

If you can't sustain 4 hours/day, switch to **5-week balanced** (it assumes more prior knowledge but fewer total hours) — but you need to be honest about what you've already mastered.

Week 6 is intentionally lighter on new content. **Do not** add new problems in week 6. Use the time for:

- Polish (re-do problems from memory)

- Sleep, exercise, mental rest

This is what separates "prepared" from "peaked." Both matter on interview day.
