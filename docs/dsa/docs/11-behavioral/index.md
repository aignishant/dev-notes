## 🎯 What every behavioral round grades

| Signal | What it means | How to show |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope of impact** | Did your work matter to a measurable outcome? | Numbers: latency / revenue / users / cost. |
| **Clarity of thought** | Can you compress a complex situation into 60-90 seconds? | STAR + practice. |
| **Self-awareness** | Do you own mistakes without minimising them? | A real failure story; what you'd do differently. |
| **Team fit** | Are you someone people want to work with? | Specific stories of collaboration, not "I'm a team player". |
| **Customer / mission orientation** | Do you push for the right outcome over the easy one? | A time you flagged a customer-impact issue against pressure. |

## ❌ The most common ways to lose a behavioral round

1. **Talking too long.** 5-minute answers kill rapport. Cap at 90s, then check in.
2. **No numbers.** "It made the system faster" → forgotten. "Cut p99 from 800ms to 120ms" → remembered.
3. **Generic stories.** "Once on a project we…" — they want a specific moment, with specific people.
4. **Ego in failure stories.** "My biggest failure was caring too much" is a tell.
5. **No question for them.** Always have one. About the team, the role, recent technical decisions.
6. **Bad-mouthing.** Even mildly. Even when justified. Especially about your last manager.
7. **Mismatched register.** Saying "we crushed it" to a Microsoft principal engineer reads wrong; saying "we leveraged synergies" to a Meta E5 reads worse.

## 📚 How to use this pack

If you have **2 weeks**:

1. Day 1-2: Read [STAR](star-method.md) + [common questions](common-questions.md). Pick 8 stories.
2. Day 3-7: Use [story bank](story-bank.md) to write all 8 in STAR form. ~250 words each.
3. Day 8-10: Read your target company's section ([Amazon LP](amazon-leadership-principles.md), [Googleyness](googleyness.md), [Meta](meta-move-fast.md), or [MS/Apple/Adobe](microsoft-apple-adobe.md)) and tag each story with the values it evidences.
4. Day 11-13: Mock with a friend, recording yourself. Cap each answer at 90s.
5. Day 14: Skim [salary negotiation](salary-negotiation.md). You're ready.

If you have **2 days**: read [STAR](star-method.md) + [common questions](common-questions.md), write your 8 stories in [story-bank](story-bank.md) form, sleep on it. That's enough.
