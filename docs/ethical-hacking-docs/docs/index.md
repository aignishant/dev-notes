!!! danger "Authorized testing only"
    Every technique in this site is taught for **legal, authorized testing, defense, and education**. Using these tools or techniques against systems you do not own or do not have written permission to test is a crime in most jurisdictions — including under the **US Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)** and the **Indian IT Act 2000 §66**. Read [Legal & Ethics](00-getting-started/legal-ethics.md) before continuing.

## Why this exists

Cybersecurity learning paths are scattered across paid courses, half-finished blog posts, and YouTube videos of varying quality. This site is the resource I wish existed — a single, opinionated, deep curriculum that:

- Goes from absolute fundamentals to advanced specializations
- Pairs every offensive technique with detection and defense
- Includes ~40 production-quality Python scripts you can read, run, and adapt
- Maps directly to **US** (NSA, CISA, FBI, USCYBERCOM, DoD) and **India** (CERT-In, NCIIPC, NTRO, DRDO, I4C, DCyA) hiring profiles
- Lists the exact certifications, CTF platforms, and study hours per stage

## What's in each chapter

Most chapters follow this rhythm so you can scan or study deeply:

1. **Concept & why it matters** — clear theory, no cargo-cult definitions
2. **Attacker view** — how it's abused
3. **Defender view** — how to detect & prevent
4. **Hands-on** — commands, labs, or a Python tool
5. **Real-world references** — CVEs, breaches, MITRE ATT&CK technique IDs
6. **Self-test & further reading**

## Who this is for

- **Software engineers** moving into security
- **University students** preparing for security careers
- **Sysadmins / network engineers** transitioning to red or blue team
- **Government job aspirants** (US & India) targeting cyber roles
- **Anyone** who wants a single coherent reference to study from

## What this is *not*

- A replacement for hands-on practice — every page nudges you toward labs
- A criminal toolkit — refusing scope, getting written authorization, and reporting responsibly are part of the discipline
- A shortcut — you will need ≥ 6 months of focused work to reach the end of Phase 3 even if you push hard
