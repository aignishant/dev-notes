# Interview AI

Comprehensive interview preparation covering ML fundamentals, algorithms, deep learning, and generative AI/LLMs.

## Topics

| Section | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| **ML Fundamentals** | Core ML concepts, bias-variance, metrics, training optimization |
| **Algorithms** | Linear models, trees, SVMs, recommenders, time series |
| **Algorithms Deep Dive** | Advanced treatment of clustering, probabilistic models, kernels |
| **Deep Learning** | CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, optimization, regularization |
| **GenAI & LLMs** | Foundations, prompting, fine-tuning, inference, evaluation, safety |
